Kalyanmoy Deb

Endowed Shelby Distinguished Professor

Kalyanmoy Deb is one of the most cited researchers in optimization, evolutionary computation, multi-objective optimization, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. He is best known for developing NSGA-II and NSGA-III, algorithms that have become the standard approach to multi-objective and many-objective optimization across engineering, science, and industry.
